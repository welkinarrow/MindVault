**What changed** (b-318, b-319)

- It replaces the payment module and adds automatic retry when checkout times out (b-318, unverified).

**Known issue** (b-318, unverified)
- When a retry fires, the customer may be charged twice.
- A fix is planned for v5.4.1.
- The effect on the East China refund rate is not yet confirmed.
