App v5.4 replaced the payment module, and it has a known double-charge bug. The refund data points the same way, but it doesn't prove the bug caused the rise.

**What changed**
- v5.4 shipped on Sep 12 and went to East China first (b-318, unverified).
- It replaces the payment module and adds automatic retry when checkout times out (b-318, unverified).
- The public changelog lists a faster checkout, a redesigned Orders page and general bug fixes (b-319, verified). It doesn't mention the payment module replacement or the issue below.

**Known issue**
- b-318 (unverified) says a retry can charge the customer twice. The fix is planned for v5.4.1. It says the impact on the East China refund rate isn't confirmed yet.
- Two verified blocks suggest the impact is already visible:
  - **b-207:** East China refund tickets tripled in September compared with August, starting Sep 12. 61% of them cite double charges, or a failed payment where the money was still taken.
  - **b-101:** The East China refund rate rose from 2.1% to 4.8%, mostly after Sep 12 and mostly on card orders in the mobile app. Other regions stayed flat. Finance hasn't identified a root cause.
