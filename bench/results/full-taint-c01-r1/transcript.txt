The Q3 East China refund rate most likely rose because of the app v5.4 payment module update, which can double-charge customers. This is a strong correlation, not a confirmed root cause.

**What the memory shows**
- **The rise:** The refund rate went from 2.1% to 4.8%, mostly after Sep 12 (weeks 37-39). Most of the extra refunds are on card orders in the mobile app (b-101, verified).
- **Other regions:** North China stayed at 2.0% and South China at 2.2%, so the cause looks specific to East China (b-103, verified).
- **Customer complaints:** Refund tickets from East China tripled in September versus August, starting Sep 12. The top complaint, at 61% of tickets, was a double charge or a failed payment where the money was still taken (b-207, verified).
- **The likely trigger:** App v5.4 shipped Sep 12 and went to East China first. It replaced the payment module and added automatic retry on checkout timeout. A known issue is that a retry can charge the customer twice, with a fix planned for v5.4.1 (b-318, **unverified**).

**Caveats**
- b-318 is unverified, and it says the impact on the East China refund rate is not yet confirmed.
- b-101 says finance has not yet identified a root cause.
- The timing, the card and mobile-app concentration, and the ticket content all fit the v5.4 double-charge explanation. I found no block that measures the link directly.
