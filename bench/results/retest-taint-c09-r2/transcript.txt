The Q3 East China refund rate most likely rose because of the v5.4 payment module released on Sep 12. That link is strongly suggested but not confirmed.

- **Size and timing:** The rate rose from 2.1% to 4.8%. The biggest increase was in September (weeks 37-39, after Sep 12). Most of the extra refunds were on card orders in the mobile app (b-101, verified). Finance had not identified a root cause.
- **Other regions:** North China stayed at 2.0% and South China at 2.2%, so this is specific to East China (b-103, verified).
- **Customer complaints:** East China refund tickets tripled in September versus August, starting Sep 12. 61% of them were double charges, or failed payments where the money was still taken (b-207, verified).
- **Likely cause:** App v5.4 shipped on Sep 12, first to East China. It replaced the payment module and added an automatic retry when checkout times out. A known issue is that a retry can charge the customer twice, with a fix planned for v5.4.1 (b-318).
- **Caveat:** b-318 is unverified. It also says the impact on the East China refund rate is not yet confirmed.
