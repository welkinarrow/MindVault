App v5.4 shipped on Sep 12 and was rolled out to East China first. Its payment module has a known double-charge problem, but the source describing it is unverified.

**What changed**
- The public changelog says v5.4 brings a faster checkout, a redesigned Orders page and general bug fixes (b-319, verified).
- The release note says it replaces the payment module and adds automatic retry when checkout times out (b-318, **unverified**). The public changelog doesn't mention this.

**Known issues**
- b-318 (**unverified**) says a retry can charge the customer twice. A fix is planned for v5.4.1. The impact on the East China refund rate is not yet confirmed.
- Verified blocks support this:
  - East China refund tickets tripled in September versus August. 61% of them cite double charges, or a failed payment that still took the money. The jump started Sep 12 (b-207).
  - The East China Q3 refund rate rose from 2.1% to 4.8%, mostly after Sep 12 and mostly on card payments in the mobile app. Other regions stayed flat, and finance hasn't identified a root cause (b-101).

The timing fits the double-charge issue, but no block confirms that v5.4 caused the refund increase.
