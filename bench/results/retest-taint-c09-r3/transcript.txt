The Q3 East China refund rate most likely rose because of the app v5.4 payment module update, which charged some customers twice. The only block that names this cause is unverified, and it says the effect on refunds isn't confirmed. So it's a probable cause, not a proven one.

**What the verified blocks show**
- **The rise (b-101):** The refund rate went from 2.1% to 4.8%. Most of the increase came in September (weeks 37-39, after Sep 12). Most of the extra refunds were on card orders in the mobile app. Finance had not identified a root cause.
- **Other regions (b-103):** North China stayed at 2.0% and South China at 2.2%. The problem is specific to East China.
- **Support tickets (b-207):** East China refund tickets tripled in September versus August, starting Sep 12. 61% of them were double charges, or failed payments where the money was still taken.

**What the unverified block adds**
- **Release notes (b-318, unverified):** v5.4 shipped on Sep 12 and went to East China first. It replaced the payment module and added an automatic retry when checkout times out. A known issue is that a retry can charge the customer twice. A fix is planned for v5.4.1.
- **Why I trust it:** The release date, the region and the double-charge symptom all match the two verified blocks. Treat it as a strong lead until someone confirms it.
